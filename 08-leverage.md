## The leverage ladder

For any repeated work: **session** (did I do it well once?) → **saved prompt** (is the ask stable?) → **template** (is the shape stable?) → **skill or automation** (is the whole workflow stable?) → **compound** (does it now pay in every subsequent session?). Only climb when the next rung earns its complexity. A template that decays is worse than no template.
